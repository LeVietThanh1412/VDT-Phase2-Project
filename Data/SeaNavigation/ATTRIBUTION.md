The regional GeoJSON, buffered geometry, raster navigation map and diagnostic images are derived from that source. Source checksum, geographic extent and processing parameters are retained in `source_manifest.json` and the runtime dataset. Preserve attribution with any distribution of the derived data; observe ODbL share-alike obligations for distributed derivative databases.

The data describes OSM land/coastline boundaries. It does not describe safe water depths, tides, real vessel clearance, navigational restrictions or reliable maritime routes. This is a game simulation navigation dataset.
