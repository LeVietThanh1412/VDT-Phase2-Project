## Buoyancy and steering follow-up (2026-11-01)

This is a targeted correctness/handling change after R4, rather than a performance optimization or another Tools/Docs cleanup. Idle/firing measurements exposed excessive heave/roll. `ShipMovementComponent` now applies passive vertical and roll/pitch resistance in PrePhysics, with water/death gates and no pose/velocity assignments. Water's synchronous path reads the selected runtime hull profile; the engine sprung-mass helper normalizes support weights after layout/COM changes, avoiding the enabled-mask cache retaining default weights of one. Larger lighter-hull supports compensate spherical volume through displacement settings.

The initial inertia tuning regressed steering. UE's inertia scale represents equivalent dimensions: increasing X/Y also increases yaw inertia. The correction uses X/Y=1 for all three profiles, Z=20 for Small and Z=6 for Medium/Large, with original mass/drive/rudder scales retained. Dedicated bidirectional Enhanced Input tests now cover every profile. Idle tests measure pitch oscillation separately from the original hull's approximately one-degree authored trim. Modified production files: `ShipMovementComponent.h/.cpp`, `ShipPhysicsProfile.h/.cpp` and the three profile data assets. Validation and scoped limitations: `Docs/Testing/BUOYANCY_STEERING_QA.md` and its compact PIE evidence.
